About Mother Land Public School

Mother Land Public School aims to support clear communication between school and families. The digital campus portal is intended to help parents follow their child's school updates.

Where enabled by the school, teachers and office staff can use the portal to manage attendance, homework, fees and report cards.

  • Login access for students, parents and authorised staff
  • Responsive layout for phones, tablets and computers
  • School information and portal access in one place
