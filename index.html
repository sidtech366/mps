About Mother Land Public School

Mother Land Public School believes good teaching and clear communication go together. Our digital campus lets parents follow their child's progress without waiting for the next parent-teacher meeting.

Teachers mark attendance and upload homework once. Parents see it the same day. The school office manages fees, receipts and report cards from a single system, so there are fewer queues and fewer mistakes.

  • Simple login for students, parents, teachers and office staff
  • Works on any phone, tablet or computer
  • Records are kept safe and visible only to the right people

Frequently asked questions

How do parents log in to the portal?

Press the Student / Parent Login button and sign in with the ID and password given by the school office.

Can I check my child's fee dues online?

Yes. After login, the Fees section shows pending dues, paid amounts and past receipts that you can print.

Where can I see exam results and report cards?

Term-wise marks and report cards appear in the Results section once the school publishes them.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the school office with your child's name and class. The office will reset your password.

Ready to open your dashboard?

Sign in to see today's attendance, homework, fees and results.
